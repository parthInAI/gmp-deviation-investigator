> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To make sure changes to equipment, procedures, software, materials and processes are assessed and approved before they are made.

## 2 Scope
All GMP-relevant changes in Fermentation Suite F2, including changes to the process monitoring model and its alarm limits.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 The change owner raises the change request and the impact assessment.
3.2 QA approves before implementation and confirms completion.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 A change request describes the change, the reason, the affected equipment and documents, and the risk to product quality.
4.2 No change is implemented before QA approval, except emergency changes, which are approved retrospectively within 2 working days.
4.3 After implementation the change owner verifies that the change worked and had no unintended effect.
4.4 The process monitoring model is static. Retraining it, changing its signals or changing its alarm limits is a change: the new version is tested against acceptance criteria approved in advance before it is used.
4.5 Instrument replacements of the same make and model are like-for-like changes and need a calibration record only.

## 5 Records
Change request; impact assessment; QA approval; verification record.
