> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To keep broth pH at the setpoint and to make sure the pH measurement can be trusted.

## 2 Scope
pH probe AE-220, pH controller AIC-220, base pump P-221 and acid pump P-222 on FV-201.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators respond to pH alarms.
3.2 QC laboratory measures offline pH on every scheduled broth sample.
3.3 Instrument technicians calibrate AE-220 before every batch.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 Setpoint pH 6.50, control band 6.45–6.55. The controller doses base (P-221) when pH falls and acid (P-222) when it rises.
4.2 AE-220 is calibrated with two buffers (pH 4.01 and 7.00) before sterilisation. Slope must be 95–102%.
4.3 Every offline broth sample is compared with the online pH. A difference greater than 0.10 pH units means the probe may be drifting: raise a deviation and inform the shift supervisor.
4.4 A drifting probe and a real pH change look the same to the controller: in both cases it doses acid or base against the reading. The offline comparison in 4.3 is the only routine way to tell them apart. Investigations must state which of the two was confirmed and how.
4.5 If base consumption rises or falls by more than 30% against the batch profile with no change in feed rate, check AE-220 first.
4.6 A probe may only be replaced or re-standardised during a batch under change control (SOP-QA-012).

## 5 Records
Calibration record; batch processing record; offline sample results (LIMS).
