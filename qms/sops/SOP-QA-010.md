> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To make sure every departure from an approved procedure, specification or process profile is recorded, assessed, investigated and closed.

## 2 Scope
All GMP activities in Fermentation Suite F2.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Whoever observes a deviation reports it before the end of the shift.
3.2 The area owner leads the investigation.
3.3 QA classifies the deviation, approves the investigation and closes the record. Only QA can close a deviation.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 A deviation record is opened in the QMS with: date and time, batch, equipment, what was observed, immediate actions.
4.2 QA classifies it within 1 working day: minor (no possible product impact), major (possible product impact) or critical (likely product impact or patient risk).
4.3 Major and critical deviations require a root cause investigation (SOP-QA-011) and a product impact assessment before the batch can be dispositioned.
4.4 Investigations should be completed within 30 calendar days. Extensions need a documented reason approved by QA.
4.5 The investigation report states: the event, the evidence reviewed (with references to data, records and procedures), the root cause or most likely root cause, the product impact, and the CAPA.
4.6 An investigation draft prepared with the help of software, including AI tools, is treated as the investigator's working draft. The named investigator remains responsible for its content and QA approval follows the same process.
4.7 Trends: QA reviews all deviations monthly for recurrence by equipment and root-cause category.

## 5 Records
Deviation record in the QMS; investigation report; QA approval.

## 6 References
SOP-QA-011 Root cause analysis and CAPA; SOP-QA-012 Change control; EU GMP Chapter 1.
