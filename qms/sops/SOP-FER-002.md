> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To control the feed of sterile sugar solution to FV-201 according to the fed-batch recipe.

## 2 Scope
Feed pump P-210, flow meter FI-211, feed tank T-210 and the feed line to FV-201.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators verify the feed profile at the start of each shift.
3.2 Engineering calibrates FI-211 every 6 months and maintains P-210.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 The sugar feed rate follows the recipe profile (normal range 15–105 L/h; rises during the production phase, then held).
4.2 At each shift start the operator compares the feed totaliser with the change in feed tank T-210 weight. A difference above 3% is reported as a deviation.
4.3 A feed rate that departs from the recipe profile for more than 10 minutes without an operator entry is a deviation.
4.4 Too much sugar shows as a rise in oxygen uptake and off-gas CO2 and a fall in DO; too little shows the opposite. The feed rate signal itself must always be checked first, because a pump fault and a sensor fault produce different investigations.
4.5 After any maintenance on P-210, the feed rate is verified against T-210 weight loss over 1 hour before the pump is returned to service.
4.6 Feed solution batches are released by QC before use; the feed solution batch number is recorded in the batch record.

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; feed tank weight log; P-210 maintenance log.
