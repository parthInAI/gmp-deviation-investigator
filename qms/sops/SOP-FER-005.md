## 1 Purpose
To keep the broth mixed and oxygen transferred by running the FV-201 agitator correctly.

## 2 Scope
Agitator motor M-240, variable speed drive VSD-240, speed sensor SI-240, mechanical seal.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators start the agitator and respond to drive alarms.
3.2 Engineering maintains the motor, drive and mechanical seal.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 The agitator runs at the recipe speed for the whole batch. Speed is not changed during a batch except under supervisor approval.
4.2 An agitator trip stops oxygen transfer: DO falls quickly while the aeration rate stays unchanged. This pattern distinguishes an agitation problem from an aeration problem (SOP-FER-001 §4.3), where aeration rate itself changes.
4.3 After any trip the operator restarts the agitator within 5 minutes if the drive allows, records the stop and restart times, and raises a deviation if the stop exceeded 5 minutes.
4.4 Seal water leaks are reported immediately: they are a contamination risk (see SOP-QC-020).
4.5 Motor current trending above 110% of the batch profile indicates high broth viscosity or a mechanical problem and is reported to Engineering.

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; drive alarm log; equipment logbook FV-201.
