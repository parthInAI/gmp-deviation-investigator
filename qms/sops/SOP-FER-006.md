> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To control the feed of phenylacetic acid (PAA) precursor and soybean oil to FV-201.

## 2 Scope
PAA pump P-250 with flow meter FI-251; oil pump P-255 with flow meter FI-256.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators verify both feed profiles each shift.
3.2 QC releases each PAA and oil batch before use.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 PAA is fed during the production phase (normal 0–11 L/h). Too much PAA is toxic to the culture; too little limits penicillin formation.
4.2 Oil is fed continuously (normal 23–35 L/h) as a second carbon source and antifoam.
4.3 A change in PAA or oil feed has a slow effect on online signals (hours, mainly on oxygen uptake and CO2). These feeds are rarely the first signal to move in a fast process upset.
4.4 Feed totalisers are reconciled against tank levels each shift; a difference above 5% is a deviation.

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; feed tank logs.
