## 1 Purpose
To detect and investigate microbial contamination of a fermentation batch.

## 2 Scope
FV-201 broth samples, seed fermenters, feed solutions and sterile boundaries (filters, seals, valves).

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 QC Microbiology tests broth samples (microscopy, plating).
3.2 The investigator reviews process data and the sterile boundary.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 Broth is sampled for microscopy and plating every 24 hours and whenever contamination is suspected.
4.2 Contamination usually shows in process data as a gradual change: rising oxygen uptake and CO2 with no change in feeds, a falling pH trend that the controller corrects with more base, and later foaming. A sudden step in one input signal is not typical of contamination.
4.3 Contamination is only confirmed by a positive microbiology result. Process data alone can raise the suspicion but cannot confirm it.
4.4 When contamination is confirmed, check in order: sterile air filter integrity, agitator seal, sample valve, feed solution sterility, seed train.

## 5 Records
Microbiology results (LIMS); investigation report.
