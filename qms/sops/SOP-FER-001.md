> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To describe how sterile air is supplied to fermenter FV-201 and how dissolved oxygen (DO) is kept within limits during a production batch.

## 2 Scope
Applies to all penicillin production batches in FV-201, from inoculation to harvest.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Fermentation operators run the aeration system and respond to alarms.
3.2 The shift supervisor approves any manual change to the aeration setpoint.
3.3 Engineering maintains the air compressor, sterile filters and the mass flow controller FIC-201.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 The aeration rate follows the recipe profile in the batch record (normal range 42–75 L/h in this process). It may only be changed manually with supervisor approval, recorded in the batch record.
4.2 Mass flow controller FIC-201 must agree with the independent flow meter FI-202 within 5%. A larger difference is a deviation: the delivered air flow is not known.
4.3 A drop in aeration rate is normally seen first as a fall in off-gas CO2 and a rise in off-gas O2 fraction, followed within about an hour by falling DO and oxygen uptake rate.
4.4 If DO falls below 8 mg/L, or the multivariate process monitor alarms with aeration among the top contributing signals, the operator checks in this order: FIC-201 setpoint versus output; FI-202 reading; sterile filter differential pressure (PDI-203); compressor status.
4.5 Sterile air filter differential pressure above 0.35 bar indicates a blocked filter. Filters are changed only under change control (SOP-QA-012) and the change is recorded in the equipment logbook.
4.6 Any loss of aeration longer than 30 minutes must be reported as a deviation (SOP-QA-010).

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; equipment logbook FV-201; alarm log of the process monitor.
