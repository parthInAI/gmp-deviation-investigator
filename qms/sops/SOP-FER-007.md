> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To maintain head pressure on FV-201 and keep the off-gas analyser reliable.

## 2 Scope
Back-pressure valve PV-260, pressure transmitter PT-260, off-gas mass spectrometer AE-265 (CO2 and O2).

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators respond to pressure alarms.
3.2 Instrument technicians calibrate AE-265 weekly with certified gas.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 Head pressure follows the recipe (normal 0.6–1.1 bar). It raises oxygen solubility, so a pressure change moves DO without any change in aeration or agitation.
4.2 A blocked or sticking back-pressure valve shows as head pressure off its profile while aeration is steady.
4.3 Off-gas CO2 and O2 are used to calculate oxygen uptake and CO2 evolution. If both shift together while all inputs are steady, check the analyser calibration before the process.
4.4 Failed analyser calibration is a deviation; the off-gas data since the last good calibration are marked as unreliable in the batch record.

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; analyser calibration log.
