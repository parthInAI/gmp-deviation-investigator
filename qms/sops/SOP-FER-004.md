> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT. Fictional site (Ardmore Biologics). Not a real procedure.

## 1 Purpose
To keep broth temperature at the setpoint during fermentation.

## 2 Scope
Temperature probes TE-230A/B, controller TIC-230, cooling water valve TV-231, heating water valve TV-232, cooling water supply from the site chilled-water loop.

## 3 Responsibilities
3.1 Operators respond to temperature alarms.
3.2 Utilities maintain the chilled-water loop and report supply interruptions.
3.3 Instrument technicians calibrate TE-230A/B every 6 months.

## 4 Procedure
4.1 Setpoint 298.0 K (24.85 °C), control band ±0.3 K. Heat from the culture is removed by cooling water; heating water is used only at low biomass.
4.2 Two independent probes (TE-230A, TE-230B) are fitted. A difference greater than 0.2 K between them is a deviation: one probe may have failed.
4.3 A rise in temperature together with a cooling water valve that is already wide open points to a cooling supply problem (loop temperature, flow or valve). A rise with the valve closing points to a measurement or controller problem.
4.4 Utilities must inform Fermentation of any chilled-water supply interruption or set-point change, and the time is recorded in the batch record.
4.5 Temperatures above 298.5 K for more than 1 hour may affect penicillin productivity and must be assessed for product impact.

## 5 Records
Batch processing record; utilities log; calibration records.
